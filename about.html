About Ishtar Gate

ISHTAR GATE PRO EQUIPMENT draws its name from the glazed-brick masterpiece of ancient Babylon— a threshold of lions, lapis, and gold. We borrow that presence for modern training floors: equipment that looks deliberate and lifts without apology.

What we make

Olympic bars, power racks, kettlebells, benches, bumpers, and conditioning tools. Specs are published plainly. Finishes lean into deep lapis blues and gold accents— a nod to the gate without turning the gym into a museum.

This demo site

Version one is fully static: HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Cart, settings, and demo orders live in your browser’s localStorage. No backend, no paid APIs, no external image CDNs for product art. Open the files locally or serve the folder with any static server.
